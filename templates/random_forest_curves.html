How to Interpret These Curves:

  • ROC Curve: Plots Sensitivity (True Positive Rate) against 1 - Specificity (False Positive Rate). A curve bowing upward toward the top-left demonstrates superior discrimination capability compared to the 45° diagonal baseline.
  • Precision-Recall Curve: Illustrates the trade-off between precision (fraction of predicted placements that are true) and recall (fraction of actual placements identified).
  • Validation Curve: Shows cross-validated performance across varying tree depths (max_depth). The peak indicates optimal model capacity without overfitting.
  • Learning Curve: Shows model convergence as training sample size scales. Narrowing gaps between training score and cross-validation score confirm low variance and high stability.
